Endre Borbáth will present first results from the project’s first work package, **Mobilisation**, at [**Social movement studies in intense times**](https://www.sns.it/it/evento/social-movement-studies-intense-times), a conference organised to honour Donatella Della Porta’s work.

The paper, **“From Valence to Cleavage: Conceptualizing the Climate Divide as Political Mobilisation Issue”**, will be presented in the **Environmental Struggles** panel on **4 June 2026**.
