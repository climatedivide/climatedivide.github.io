title: ClimateDivide at CES Dublin and EPSS Belfast
date: 2026-05-04T09:00:00+02:00
summary: The project will present new work on automated claims analysis and the climate divide at CES Dublin and EPSS Belfast.
---

The **New Climate Divide** project will be represented at two major political science conferences in June: the [**32nd International Conference of Europeanists**](https://councilforeuropeanstudies.org/conferences/upcoming-conferences/) in Dublin and the inaugural conference of the [**European Political Science Society**](https://epssnet.org/belfast-2026/) in Belfast.
<br>
Dino Noah Wildi will present **“Automated Political Claims Analysis With LLMs”**, and Endre Borbáth will present **“From Valence to Cleavage: Conceptualizing the Climate Divide as Political Mobilisation Issue”**.
