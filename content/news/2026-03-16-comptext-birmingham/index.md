Dino Noah Wildi will present **“Automated Political Claims Analysis With LLMs”** at the [**8th Annual COMPTEXT Conference 2026**](https://www.comptextconference.org/8th-annual-conference-2026/) at the University of Birmingham.
<br>
The conference takes place from **23-25 April 2026** and brings together researchers working on the quantitative and computational analysis of text, image, audio, and video as data.
