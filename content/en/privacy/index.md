## 2. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the time of access and the requested page in order to deliver the site and to keep it secure (server log files). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be processed in the USA; GitHub's parent company, Microsoft, is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Further information: [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

All fonts, scripts and style sheets used for the layout are served from this website itself. No data is sent to third-party content delivery networks or font services when you load the pages. The site search runs entirely in your browser.

## 3. Consent cookie

When you make a choice in the privacy banner, it is stored in a cookie called `cc_cookie` in your browser for six months, so that the banner does not reappear on every page. This is strictly necessary to remember your choice (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG; Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR); the cookie is not used for any other purpose.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer of every page.

## 4. Google Analytics (only with your consent)

If you accept "Statistics", this website uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google Analytics uses cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`, stored for up to two years) to recognise your browser and to produce aggregated statistics on how the site is used (for example pages visited, time spent, device type and approximate location). According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. The data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25(1) TDDDG). Google Analytics is not loaded at all unless you consent, and you can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future via "Cookie settings" in the footer. Further information: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Analytics data practices](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245).

## 5. External links

This website links to other websites (for example journals, ORCID, Google Scholar, Bluesky, LinkedIn or the team members' personal websites). No data is sent to these services unless you click such a link; after that, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

## 6. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide in order to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete it once it is no longer needed. Applications for advertised positions are handled according to the procedures of Heidelberg University described in the respective call.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg (LfDI BW).
