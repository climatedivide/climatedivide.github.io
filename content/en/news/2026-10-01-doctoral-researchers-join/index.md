We are delighted to welcome [**Angelika Juhász**]({{< relref "/authors/angelika-juhasz" >}}) and [**Miriam Runde**]({{< relref "/authors/miriam-runde" >}}) as doctoral researchers in **The New Climate Divide** at Heidelberg University.

Angelika investigates how opposing political group identities form around climate change, while Miriam examines how climate change issues shape citizens’ political participation in Europe.
