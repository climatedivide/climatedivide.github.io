---

The **New Climate Divide** project will be represented at two political science conferences next week: the [**32nd International Conference of Europeanists**](https://councilforeuropeanstudies.org/conferences/upcoming-conferences/) in Dublin (**16-18 June 2026**) and the [**European Political Science Society conference**](https://epssnet.org/belfast-2026/) in Belfast (**18-20 June 2026**).
<br>
At CES, two papers co-authored by Dino Noah Wildi and Endre Borbáth will be presented: **“From Valence to Cleavage: Conceptualizing the Climate Divide as Political Mobilisation Issue”** and **“Automated Political Claims Analysis With LLMs”**.
